♻ About Waste Classifier AI

An intelligent waste detection system powered by CNN Deep Learning technology.

🤖 Our Mission

Waste management is a major environmental challenge. Our AI system helps identify waste types automatically and supports better recycling decisions.

🧠 How AI Works

The system uses a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) to analyze uploaded waste images and classify them into different categories.
